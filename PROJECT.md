# AI-OS

Version: 1.0 (Development)

## Vision

AI-OS is a local-first AI operating system designed to help a solo entrepreneur build, manage, and automate multiple online businesses.

The goal is not to build a chatbot.

The goal is to build an AI company that assists with research, coding, marketing, content creation, automation, and business management.

---

## Core Principles

- Local-first whenever possible
- Modular architecture
- AI agents with specialized roles
- Human approval for sensitive actions
- Scalable to multiple businesses
- Easy to extend
- Professional software engineering practices

---

## Long-Term Goal

Allow the user to operate multiple businesses from one application.

Examples:

- Website Agency
- YouTube Automation
- Print-on-Demand
- Digital Products
- SaaS
- AI Automation Agency
